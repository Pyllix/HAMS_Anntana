# Public asset images and short-lived profile-photo read links

HAMS will deliver asset images through stable versioned public CDN URLs without a content-review workflow, while employee profile photos require backend-authorized signed read links expiring after five minutes; image bytes go directly from the provider to browsers, not through Render. The user accepted anonymous access to asset-image URLs, possible exposure of unintentionally included content, and use of an unexpired profile-photo link by anyone possessing it, in exchange for simple implementation and effective public asset caching; registry metadata and image mutations remain protected. Cloudinary remains behind a replaceable storage abstraction, with the known trade-off that its time-limited profile-photo download method bypasses the CDN and costs twice its delivery bandwidth; permanent signed CDN URLs must not be substituted for expiring profile-photo grants, and cache-header capabilities must be verified before promising exact values.
